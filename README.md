# MIYOYAZU DASH

A 3D dash platformer from EX Inc./HYPERION DIGITAL. Play as **Miyoyazu** — run, jump, double-jump, dash, collect coins and clear the goal across **60 stages** in six original worlds.

## Worlds
1. **STONE RUINS** — broken stairs and ancient stone (cover look)
2. **HIKARI PARK** — Tokyo-adjacent green park paths
3. **NEON PATH** — night neon walkways
4. **AUTUMN GARDEN** — warm decks and fallen leaves
5. **WINTER CLIFF** — icy ledges
6. **SPRING BLOSSOM** — petal paths and soft bridges

Ten stages per world with a real challenge ramp: early stages teach run / jump / dash and coin routes; mid stages open longer gaps and height changes; late stages shrink platforms, demand double-jumps and well-timed dashes, and layer twists under pressure. Some stages add a light twist: **TIME VEIL**, **GIANT STEP**, **LIGHT FALL**, **SKY FLIP**, or **RUSH WIND**.

## Controls
WASD move (**A** left, **D** right), Space jump / double-jump, Shift dash, Esc pause. Mouse to peek (auto camera takes back over), **C** toggles AUTO CAMERA.

## Audio
Retro energizing beds (menu + stage loops) and punchy 90s-style blips — square/pulse/noise, not cutesy.

## Auto camera (1.0.2)
The gameplay camera frames Miyoyazu automatically: it swings behind her run direction (harder into sideways turns, steadier in the air), leads the frame along her velocity, tilts down to show the landing when she falls and lifts on a rising jump, eases back behind her when idle, and softly pulls in (and slightly up) when a wall or platform blocks the view. Moving the mouse lets you peek; the auto camera eases back in after ~1.4 s.

## Graphics
Third-person / over-shoulder camera, filmic colour grade and grain, RTX / EX HD modes. No bloom.

## Windows
Per-user installer (no admin) or portable zip. Saves in `%APPDATA%\EX Inc\MIYOYAZU DASH`.
